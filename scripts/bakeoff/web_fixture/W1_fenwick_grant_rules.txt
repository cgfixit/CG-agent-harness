Fenwick Foundation - Community Library Grant Program rules (2026 cycle)
- Grants match up to 50% of equipment costs, to a maximum grant of $5,000 per applicant per year.
- Annual report due March 31 for the prior grant year.
- Cash membership dues are not eligible as matching funds.
- Example applicant types: neighborhood tool libraries (such as Harbor Street style co-ops), seed libraries, toy libraries.
