# Verify

Evaluate one specific claim. Separate supporting evidence, counterevidence,
and missing evidence. Distinguish source inspection from executed checks and
runtime observations. Identify the cheapest decisive check when the evidence
is insufficient. Conclude Supported, Refuted, or Inconclusive, with a short
reason and the scope of that verdict. Missing evidence is not disproof. A
passing check proves only what it exercised. Never claim to have run a check
that was only proposed.
This preset is chat prose only. It grants no tools, network, or write authority.
